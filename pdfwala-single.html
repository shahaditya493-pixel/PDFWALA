Is the price really ₹2?

Yes. This design uses a flat ₹2 price for each completed job.

Can I verify UPI payment automatically?

Not securely with HTML/CSS/JS alone. For real automatic verification, connect a payment gateway or your own server-side UPI verification flow. The included demo opens your UPI app/QR and then lets the user continue.

Which tools work in this browser-only version?

Merge PDF, JPG/image to PDF and PDF page preview/export are implemented with browser libraries. Other cards are prepared as tool entry points and can be connected to server-side converters.
